Each hexagon is an H3 resolution-6 cell, about 36 km² on average worldwide (about 40 km² here, near the equator). The hexagon is a common index for joining observations, not a claim that any source measures at that precision. Every value below shows the grid or points it was measured on and how many of them fell in the cell.

Values are shown one layer at a time. Nothing is combined into a score, and nothing is ranked. Hatched cells have no value, and the inspector says why.
